# 🔧 WrenchCall AI — 24/7 AI Lead Generation Agent for Plumbers & Handymen

> **"Never lose a $1,500 job just because your hands are under a sink."**

WrenchCall AI is a high-converting, responsive landing page and web application showcasing a 24/7 conversational voice AI receptionist specifically engineered for residential trade contractors (plumbers, drain cleaners, handymen, and home service technicians).

---

## 🎯 The Business Opportunity & Problem Solved

- **The Contractor's Dilemma**: When a contractor is under a crawlspace, soldering copper pipe, up on a ladder, or driving, they cannot safely answer the phone.
- **The Homeowner Reality**: **67% of homeowners** hire the very first service contractor who answers. Unanswered calls don't leave voicemails—they hang up and call the next listing on Google.
- **The Solution**: WrenchCall AI answers on the 2nd ring, speaks fluent trade terminology, calms panicking homeowners, triages emergencies (instructing shut-off valves for floods), captures job photos via SMS, quotes baseline dispatch fees, and books jobs directly into field service software (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Google Calendar).

## 🥊 Competitor Analysis: How WrenchCall AI Wins

### 1. Sameday.ai
- **What they do**: Inbound voice AI receptionist & workflow automation certified for ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber.
- **Their Pricing**: $449/mo (Launch, 500 mins) to $789/mo (Scale, 1,000 mins).
- **Top Features**: Emergency triage, real-time FSM appointment booking, bilingual Spanish/English on one line, multi-unit dispatching.
- **Where WrenchCall AI Wins**: Sameday targets large commercial operations and costs a minimum of $449/mo. WrenchCall AI gives solo handymen and 1-3 truck plumbers the exact same core capabilities for **only $79–$149/mo** with self-serve 8-minute onboarding.

### 2. ZyraTalk
- **What they do**: AI-powered customer communication platform (voice receptionist, webchat widget, outbound SMS reminders) for field service businesses.
- **Their Pricing**: Starts around $500/month (requires booking a sales demo).
- **Top Features**: 1st-ring answering, "Where's my tech?" inquiry handling, FSM calendar write-back, automated review collection, and outbound estimate chase.
- **Where WrenchCall AI Wins**: ZyraTalk requires a locked-in enterprise contract and sales demos. WrenchCall offers transparent public pricing, a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, and specialized leak/damage photo intake links via SMS.

### 3. OnCrew.ai
- **What they do**: Budget-oriented AI receptionist for home-service contractors (HVAC, plumbing, roofing).
- **Their Pricing**: $49/mo (250 mins), $149/mo (500 mins), $349/mo (1,500 mins).
- **Top Features**: Auto-trains receptionist by scraping contractor's website, emergency triage with 90-second on-call SMS alerts, spam call screening, Housecall Pro & Google Calendar sync.
- **Where WrenchCall AI Wins**: While OnCrew offers great entry pricing, WrenchCall AI provides deeper interactive customer tools—including real-time contractor calendar booking, live emergency phone bridge calls, caller photo uploads, and an interactive customer-facing ROI simulator.

   - Visitors can select from 3 realistic trade scenarios:
     - 🚨 *Emergency Burst Water Pipe* (Denver, CO)
     - 🔨 *Handyman Drywall & Door Repair* (Austin, TX)
     - ♨️ *Plumbing Tankless Water Heater Quote* (Tampa, FL)

   - Live AI Lead Dossier extraction (Caller name, phone, address, severity, and booked slot).

3. **Billing Period Switcher**:
   - Seamlessly toggle between Monthly and Annual billing (with 20% discount badge and animated price adjustment).

4. **14-Day Free Trial Modal**:
   - Lead capture form with trade selector and mobile number verification.
   - Interactive success state simulating instant telephone provisioning.

5. **Field Service Integrations Showcase**:
   - Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Google Calendar, Zapier, QuickBooks.

6. **Contractor Objections & FAQ Accordion**:
   - Overcomes fears regarding robotic voices, existing phone numbers, emergency flooding, and pricing quotes.
